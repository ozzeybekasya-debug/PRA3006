# PRA3006 - Programming in Life Sciences
## Initial Research Question: 
To what extent do the pathways in the cellular energy metabolism have statistically significantly higher gene overlap with the pathways belonging to the same biological metabolism compared to the pathways from other metabolisms?

## Possible New Approach:
1. Identify the genes in the pathways - focusing on the ones that encode enzymes involved in the reactions.
2. Calculate gene overlap and the statistical significance to consider pathway size.
3. Retrieve the EC numbers (four digit number showing the which reactions enzymes participate in) of the enzymes.
4. The EC similarity analysis will particularly focus on enzymes encoded by non-shared genes. For example, if two pathways share 3 out of 5 enzyme-encoding genes, the EC numbers of the enzymes in both pathways will be compared. The enzymes encoded by the shared genes will generally have the same EC numbers, whereas those encoded by the non-shared genes may differ. The extent of these differences will then be analyzed.
